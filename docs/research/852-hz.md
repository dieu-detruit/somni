# 852 Hz: pitch, beat rate and recovery evidence

Reviewed: 2026-11-10. Targeted primary-source search, not a systematic review.

## Distinct variables

852 Hz is an audible tone frequency. The current introduction uses 165/179-Hz tones with a difference of 14 Hz. A single 852-Hz sine wave has no 14-Hz beat. Combining 852 and 866 Hz could create a 14-Hz difference at a higher pitch, but that is a separate, unvalidated intervention. A higher tone frequency is not a higher dose of the current beat.

## Primary studies located

- [Kumar, Abhayapala and Samarasinghe, 2022 — A Preliminary Investigation on Frequency Dependant Cues for Human Emotions](https://doi.org/10.3390/acoustics4020028). **Access: full-text methods/results.** Thirty-seven people rated perceived emotions for twelve pure tones, including 852 Hz, presented for 20 seconds each. Higher-frequency tones tended toward lower calm ratings and higher anger ratings. This measures impressions of sound, not recovery from cognitive fatigue, overnight sleep or next-morning restoration. It does not demonstrate a special restorative property of 852 Hz.
- [Presley et al., 2026 — Evaluating the Effects of Pure Tone Solfeggio Frequencies on Skin Conductivity](https://doi.org/10.31275/20263661), available in the [publisher's complete issue](https://journalofscientificexploration.org/index.php/jse/issue/view/115/53). **Access: publisher PDF methods/results/discussion.** Eighteen participants heard six frequencies and a combined exposure. Authors report higher skin conductivity at 741/852 Hz and speculate about therapeutic uses. Increased autonomic arousal is not established relaxation or fatigue recovery. Fixed frequency order, small sample, lack of an adequate comparator and the absence of sleep/cognitive-recovery outcomes limit the inference. Speculative healing interpretations are not supported by the measured endpoint.

## Current conclusion

The targeted search located direct exposure studies, but no trial establishing that 852-Hz audio improves cognitive-fatigue recovery or next-morning restfulness. This is an evidence gap, not proof that a particular listener cannot find it pleasant. Any future app option should distinguish a plain tone from an 852-Hz carrier plus a separately specified beat, preserve comfortable volume, and record the actual signal. The adaptive-output release does not change carrier frequencies or add an 852-Hz preset.
